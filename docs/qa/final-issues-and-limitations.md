## Technical limitations (by design)

- No CMS, authentication, or claims submission backend — claim CTA opens existing mua.rw form.
- Quote panel remains a homepage lead-in; not a live rating engine.
- Investor materials link out to published group resources on mua.rw.
- Career vacancy/spontaneous application CTAs point to existing mua.rw journeys.
- Image set is limited to Phase 0 inventory assets; some company pages reuse news photography as institutional atmosphere.

## Visual / UX residuals (non-blocking)

- Plain navy heroes (no photography) are intentionally tighter but less atmospheric than image heroes.
- Business motor pages that “mirror” personal families remain thin by content truth — not padded with invented covers.
- Dense business mega-menu may benefit from grouping in a later IA pass.
- Full automated visual regression suite not introduced (manual viewport captures only).
